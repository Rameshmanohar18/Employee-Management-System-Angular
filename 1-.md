## 07. Authentication & Session Management

* **File Path**: [`src/app/solids/login/login.component.ts`](file:///d:/Ramesh/SH_FE/sh_fe/src/app/solids/login/login.component.ts)
* **Authentication Pipeline**:
  1. **Dynamic Gateway URL Resolution**:
     * `ngOnInit()` reads `assets/config.json` (or `assets/configghana.json`).
     * Sets `localStorage.setItem('SOLIDS_MASTER_URL', post.api_master_url)`.
  2. **Credential Sign-In**:
     * `submitLogin()` posts `{ mailId, password, compId }` to `/api/auth/login`.
     * Receives `{ apiKey, userId, roleId, compId, locationName, applPrintOptions }`.
  3. **MFA / OTP Flow**:
     * If OTP is enforced, switches to `showOTP = true` and posts `{ hashKey, otp }` to `/api/auth/login/otp`.
  4. **State Storage**:
     * Persists token: `localStorage.setItem('SOLIDS_DATA', JSON.stringify(storeObj))` and `localStorage.setItem('SOLIDS_Token', apiKey)`.
  5. **Branch Clearance & Landing Navigation**:
     * Checks `ADMIN_ACCESS` generic master. If user is listed, sets `localStorage('ALQ_ACCESS', 'ALL')`.
     * Resolves role menus and redirects to the user's primary workspace (`/customer-detail` or `/invoice/list`).

## 11. Salesman Geo-Tracking Engine

* **File Paths**:
  * [`src/app/app.component.ts`](file:///d:/Ramesh/SH_FE/sh_fe/src/app/app.component.ts#L289-L677)
  * [`src/app/service/geocoding.service.ts`](file:///d:/Ramesh/SH_FE/sh_fe/src/app/service/geocoding.service.ts)
* **Architecture & Telemetry Pipeline**:
  1. **Master Toggle & Interval Resolution**: Gated by `GEO_TRACK_ENABLE_YN` and `GEO_TRACK_INTERVAL` generic masters.
  2. **Background Logging (`sendLocationLog`)**:
     * Acquires device GPS coordinates via `navigator.geolocation`.
     * Reverse geocodes coordinates to street addresses via Google Maps Geocoder API.
     * Captures battery level (`navigator.getBattery()`) and client IP (`api.ipify.org`).
     * Posts telemetry to `POST /api/map-api-log/save`.
  3. **Route Inactivity Timeout**:
     * Enforces `routeTimeoutMultiplier`. If a salesman remains on one route for multiple intervals without movement, tracking pauses automatically to preserve battery life and minimize API costs.
